Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up?

You can sign up using your university email address (ending in .edu.hk or similar). We'll send you a verification code to confirm your email, then you can complete your profile.

Who can use BridgeU?

BridgeU is exclusively for verified university students. You must have a valid university email address to register.

How does matching work?

You can browse profiles of other students who match your preferences. When you both like each other, it's a match! You'll then be able to start chatting.

Is my information secure?

Yes! We take your privacy seriously. Your personal information is encrypted and secure. You control what information you share on your profile.

Can I change my preferences?

Absolutely! You can update your profile, interests, and matching preferences at any time from the Edit Profile section.

What is the AI Assistant?

Our AI Assistant helps you with conversation starters and connection advice. It's designed to make chatting easier and more engaging.

How do I report inappropriate behavior?

User safety is our priority. You can report any user or content through the app's reporting feature, or contact us directly at the email below.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, you can permanently delete your account at any time from the Settings menu. This action is irreversible.

Safety Guidelines

  • Never share personal information (phone number, address) until you trust someone
  • Meet in public places for first dates
  • Tell a friend about your plans
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, it probably is
  • Report suspicious or inappropriate behavior immediately
  • Never send money or gift cards to anyone
